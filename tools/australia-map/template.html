Australia Pin Map

Paste GPS coordinates to drop pins on a 3D terrain map and see the nearest Green Light Point of Presence. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to tilt and rotate.

Add coordinates

One location per line: lat, lon, label. Decimal degrees or degrees-minutes-seconds with N/S/E/W both work, and so does a Google Maps link. Or click the map to drop a pin there.
